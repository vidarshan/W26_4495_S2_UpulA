Eco-Clean project references

An Affordable Digital Transformation Enabler for Workforce Scheduling in Small Businesses: Eco-Clean

Vidarshan Rathnayake | Upul Atapattu



Eco-Clean is a digital scheduling platform designed for small service businesses such as cleaning companies, which improves appointment management, staff coordination, availability tracking, leave handling, and timesheet workflows. Its novelty lies in combining a centralized web platform with a staff-focused Progressive Web Application (PWA) for delivering a practical, low-cost solution.
